Why Choose Endurocide?

Dual-action technology traps and kills pathogens. 100% recyclable polypropylene fabric. Flame retardant to international standards.

Endurocide® Antimicrobial Curtains are the first of their kind to be impregnated with a patented liquid formulation that traps and kills bacteria, fungi, and spores on the fabric surface. This unique "Trap & Kill" technology ensures that pathogens are not only prevented from multiplying but are actively destroyed upon contact, significantly reducing the risk of cross-contamination in healthcare environments.

Unlike standard hospital curtains which can become contaminated within hours of hanging, Endurocide® curtains provide continuous protection for up to 24 months. This extended lifespan not only improves infection control outcomes but also delivers substantial cost savings by reducing the frequency of curtain changeovers, laundry costs, and associated labor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do Endurocide® curtains work?

The fabric is treated with a biocide that remains active for the lifetime of the curtain. When pathogens come into contact with the fabric, the biocide penetrates the cell wall, disrupting its function and killing the microorganism.

Are they effective against COVID-19?

Yes, Endurocide® curtains have been independently tested and proven effective against enveloped viruses, including H1N1 and surrogates for SARS-CoV-2.

Are the curtains recyclable?

Yes, our curtains are made from 100% polypropylene and are fully recyclable. We are committed to sustainability and reducing medical waste in New Zealand landfills.

Do they meet fire safety standards?

Absolutely. All Endurocide® curtains are flame retardant and meet international fire safety standards, including BS 5867 Part 2 Type C.
